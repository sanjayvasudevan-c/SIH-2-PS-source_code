# 05 — External research and references

Research was conducted on 31 Oct 2026 with web search, preferring primary sources. **Reliability** is marked for each source:
- **P**: primary (standard body, government, official vendor documentation).
- **S**: secondary (reputable press or law-firm summary).
- **T**: third-party or unverified.

Each finding states whether it changes an architecture decision.

> v3 says its references were "carried forward, not re-fetched". This document re-checks them and adds new ones.

## 3. Comparable systems: what to borrow

| System | Pattern worth borrowing | Not to copy |
|---|---|---|
| NAD/DigiLocker | Institution-only writes; consent for third-party access; issuer onboarding | Their document-centric model (we need in-progress enrolment state) |
| UK MoJ Splink | EM-estimated, explainable linkage at 10⁷ scale | Spark infrastructure (DuckDB suffices here) |
| EUDI / EBSI | SD-JWT VC for diplomas; accreditation itself as a credential | DID ledgers / blockchain registries |
| AISHE | National HE reporting cadence and formats | Annual aggregate model (the problem SIH26263 exists to fix) |
| Certificate Transparency | Signed checkpoints, public verifiability | A full Merkle log before third-party proofs are required |
